# Motion blueprint

Proposed timings serve as starting values for device testing. Use native scrolling with one scoped GSAP lifecycle per interactive feature. Keep meaningful HTML visible before motion initializes.

| Beat                    | Trigger and direction                                                                                                                                                               | Reduced motion and small screens                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hero                    | On initial entry, a photographic crop and wordmark settle over roughly 600–800 ms. The headline and CTA are immediately readable and operable.                                      | Show final composition. Avoid full-screen moving media on narrow devices.                                                                |

## Ownership and cleanup

- Scroll progress drives the camera only during the entry sequence. Explicit control cancels the active camera tween and releases scroll ownership. Subsequent selection starts from the current camera pose, and the most recent command wins.
- Scope timelines, media queries, and ScrollTriggers to their feature and revert them on unmount. Cancel outstanding camera tweens and disconnect resize observers and context event listeners. Resize refits the current destination. Refresh scroll geometry after relevant image, font, or layout changes.
- Prefer transform and opacity for DOM animation. Avoid pinning as a baseline, perpetual idle motion, scroll-jacking and competing scroll libraries. Listen for changes to the reduced-motion preference.
- Verify entry and reverse scroll, interrupted selection, return, resizing mid-transition, reduced-motion changes, and remounts. Check that focus remains visible and a keyboard user can exit the section normally.
