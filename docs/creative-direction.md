# Creative direction

## Enter the Hellden Universe

An editorial photography experience for Gen Z and young adults exploring creative studio sessions. The page should let visitors feel the studio's point of view, understand its layout, discover rooms, and reach a verified booking channel.

**Proposed direction.** Treat the studio like a photography issue with an architectural plate at the center. Pair a close photographic crop with oversized editorial type at entry. Let the building open into a lit cutaway, then give studio photography generous space in the gallery. Use restrained production cues such as floor labels and image credits where they convey information.

A photo-only editorial page would showcase the work with lower rendering cost but leave the studio anatomy unexplored. A continuous 3D walkthrough would increase asset requirements, imply more geometric accuracy, and complicate mobile navigation. The proposed editorial page with a focused 3D section preserves the supplied model and keeps the broader journey legible.

## Design system proposal

| Role            | Proposed treatment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Canvas and text | Ink `#151311`, warm paper `#F3E9D8`, and muted warm text `#C4B8A9` on ink. Alternate dark entry and anatomy with paper editorial sections.                                                                                                                                           |
| Accent          | Oxblood `#8F2D36` against paper, amber `#FFAD63` against ink. Check actual text and focus pairings, including overlays.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Typography      | Condensed display with Barlow Condensed as a candidate, clear body text with Inter as a candidate, and a restrained mono treatment for functional floor labels. Confirm licensing and locale coverage before self-hosting. Use fluid display sizes and keep body text at least 16px. |
| Grid            | 4 columns on small screens, 8 on tablets, 12 on desktop. Use a 4/8/16/24/32/48/64/96px spacing scale and cap reading lines around 65 characters.                                                                                                                                     |
| Photography     | Real studio images, deliberate crops, varied image scales, relevant caption or credit. Preserve natural skin tones and the visual character of the rooms.                                                                                                                            |
| Controls        | Clear button states, visible keyboard focus, 44px target size where layout allows, and selected states conveyed by text and shape as well as color.                                                                                                                                  |

## Page sequence

| Section                    | Visitor experience                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cinematic Hero             | An approved photo frames “ENTER THE HELLDEN UNIVERSE.” A visible “Explore the studio” anchor leads to Anatomy.                                              |
| Brand Manifesto            | A compact editorial passage gives voice to the studio using approved copy.                                                                                  |
| Interactive Studio Anatomy | An open-front two-floor overview exposes floor controls, room selection and the way back to the whole building. The concept-model label stays visible.      |
| Room Exploration           | A browseable index and detail panel share selection with Anatomy. Visitors can learn about a room through text, with associated photography when available. |
| Editorial Photo Gallery    | Alternate full-width images with paired crops selected for a visual relationship. Preserve reading order at narrow widths.                                  |
| Booking CTA                | One prominent route to the confirmed booking channel.                                                                                                       |
| Footer                     | Verified navigation and business information in a quiet, readable layout.                                                                                   |

Favor expressive typography, lighting, and photography. Keep interface surfaces flat and legible, and use motion only for spatial context and narrative pacing. See [motion blueprint](motion-blueprint.md) and [asset inventory](asset-inventory.md).
